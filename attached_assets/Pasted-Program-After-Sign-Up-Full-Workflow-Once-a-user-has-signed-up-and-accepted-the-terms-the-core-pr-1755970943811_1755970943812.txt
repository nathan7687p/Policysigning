Program After Sign-Up – Full Workflow

Once a user has signed up and accepted the terms, the core program functions kick in. These functions revolve around policies, job numbers, signatures, records, and printing.

1. Policy Management

Admin (or authorized staff) can create and manage different policies.

Each policy has:

Policy ID

Policy text (the actual terms)

Date created / last updated

Version number

👉 Example:

Policy 001 – “Repair Terms & Conditions”

Policy 002 – “Service Agreement for Lawn Equipment”

2. Add New Policy to a Job

When a customer brings in a machine or requires a service, the staff member creates a Job Card.

Each Job Card includes:

Unique Job Number (auto-generated or manual input)

Customer Details (Name, Contact, Email, Address, etc.)

Machine Details (Brand, Model, Serial Number, Condition)

Related Policy (choose from the saved policies in the system)

Photos of the machine (taken from phone or uploaded from computer)

👉 Example:
Job Number: J-2025-001
Customer: John Smith
Machine: Stihl Lawnmower, Model X500
Policy Attached: Policy 001 (Repair Terms)

3. Customer Signature Process

Customer is presented with the Policy + Job Card on-screen (tablet, phone, or PC).

Customer signs digitally:

Finger/stylus on touchscreen OR

Mouse input OR

Type-to-sign with authentication (less secure)

Signature is saved and bound to:

Customer record

Job Number

Policy version

👉 Example:
John signs the Repair Terms (Policy 001) for Job J-2025-001. His signature, date, and time are stored permanently.

4. Viewing & Searching

The program includes a search & filter system so staff can quickly find signed policies and jobs. Search filters include:

Job Number

Customer Name

Date Range

Machine Type / Serial Number

Policy ID / Version

Signed (Yes/No)

👉 Example:
Search “Job Number J-2025-001” → Shows John Smith’s signed policy, machine details, and photos.

5. Printing a Job Card + Policy

When a job card is completed, the program can generate a printable document that contains:

Header Information

Company Name, Logo, Contact Info

Job Number, Date, Customer Info

Machine Information

Description, Model, Serial Number

Photos of the machine (before repair/service)

Policy Section

Full text of the attached policy (Policy 001, etc.)

Version number & date

Signature Section

Customer signature

Staff signature (if required)

Date & time of signing

✅ This is all formatted neatly on one printable PDF page (or multiple if needed).

👉 Example:
A printed page shows:

Job Number J-2025-001

Customer: John Smith

Machine photo on left, description on right

Policy terms below

Signature box with John’s signed digital signature at the bottom

6. Saving & Syncing

Each signed job card with policy is saved in the local WiFi database (like explained earlier).

When on another WiFi, it syncs with that network’s database.

PDF copy of the signed job card can also be exported and emailed to the customer automatically for their records.

🚀 Summary of Features After Sign-Up

Add policies → store in system.

Create job cards → link policy + machine + customer.

Capture signatures → digital signing system.

Store & search → filter by job number, customer, or policy.

Print → one-page document with machine photo, policy text, and signatures.

Save & sync → WiFi-based network storage, optional email export.
